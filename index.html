Our story

Built around the fire, not around a trend.

Ember & Olive opened with one idea: cook honestly, over real flame, with ingredients that come from people we know. What started as a single wood-fired hearth has grown into a full dining room — but the fire is still the center of everything we make.

The room

An atmosphere built for slowing down.

Low light, oak tables, and a dining room designed around long conversations — this is a place to linger, not rush. Every seat has a view of the open hearth.

In the kitchen

Cooking that starts with the fire.

Our kitchen is built around a single wood hearth. Nothing goes out that hasn't passed near the flame — it's slower, and it's worth it.
